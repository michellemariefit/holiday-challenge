# The Holiday Challenge player

Free phone app for Michelle's holiday challenge: the rules as a daily checklist, a water counter, a week
tracker (3 weight workouts, 3 Zone 2 cardio), and the Toning Vault's Toning 3 workout with photos,
videos and timers. Every page points at The Toning Vault. Built from the toning-vault-player engine;
everything is saved on the phone under hc:*.

`SALE_URL` in vault.js is the button link (default: the Toning Vault sales page). Swap it for the Black Friday link.
