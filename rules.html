The rules

Most of it is food, because the next month is parties, events, eating and drinking more than normal. So we go low on carbs during the week, knowing the cookie parties will bring plenty.

Every day

  1. Carbs only at breakfast, and maybe one more meal. Not dinner.Rather skip the breakfast carb and have it at lunch? Fine.
  2. Protein in every meal, and at least 4 meals a day.
  3. Only eat out or order in when there's a party or event.Saves you money too. You're welcome.
  4. Half your body weight in pounds, in ounces of water, every day.
  5. A healthy fat in 2 meals a day.
  6. No wheat, gluten or processed sugar, Monday to Friday.
  7. Cut down on dairy as much as you can.
  8. Coffee? No sugar in it this month.
  9. Start the morning with warm water and half a lemon, before coffee.
  10. 10 to 12 hours between dinner and the first meal of the next day.

Every week

  1. 3 weight training workouts.Toning 3, Day 1, 2 and 3. It's in the app with photos, videos and a timer.
  2. 3 cardio workouts, Zone 2, 20 to 30 minutes.After a weight workout or on its own day. Zone 2 means you could still talk.

Michelle's notes

Carbs and protein

The carb thing shouldn't be hard. Plan ahead: nice salads, filling veggies you enjoy, and some healthy fat to fill you up. Cook extra chicken or turkey, boil eggs, so there's always protein ready. Hard time getting protein in? Shakes are a great option.

Eating out and ordering in

Hard if you're used to it during the week. Commit to eating out only for a holiday festivity or on the weekend. A home-cooked meal at least 5 times a week. You just need a plan every week, and then it's easy.

Water

Crucial. If you don't have a refillable cup big enough, and not plastic, get one. Then give yourself a number of refills a day. The Water page does that math and lets you tap them off.

Healthy fats

Avocados go with everything, so get a bunch every week. Almond butter in a shake, nuts on your veggies or salad, ghee at breakfast. Get them in. If not, you'll crave more carbs and it gets harder to stick to.
